## Deliberate exclusions and source differences

- Do not import the original novels' chronology, Nemo's ancestry, death, or island destruction into the operating base.
- Do not assert unverified island coordinates, exact research year, S.E.A. membership, secret passages, or a definite reason for the Chinese cuisine.
- The Greek origin of the volcano name, literal Latin variants, BGM absence, volcanic rock viscosity, and attraction engineering are not required to explain the port. Omit rather than overclaim.
- The Japanese Vulcania page emphasizes the conversion for visitors; English emphasizes the continuing power-station role. Describe both without treating conversion as the end of power generation.
- The D23 article displays December 2024 dates and separately mentions the film's December 23, 1954 release. Cite the article by year; do not mistake the film anniversary for the article date.
- Interpretive transitions in the story describe how the verified details connect. They do not invent machinery, staff routines, or designer intentions.
